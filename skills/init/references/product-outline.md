# Product record outline

Use only sections that carry actual information. Preserve an existing useful structure. Facts come from the brief, user decisions, or cited project evidence; assumptions and open choices remain labeled.

- **Platform and stack:** observed deployment/runtime and framework constraints, or explicit unresolved choices. Mobile web is distinct from native UI.
- **Users:** primary audience, situation, and job; secondary audiences when established.
- **Product purpose:** what the product enables and how success is understood.
- **Positioning:** a distinctive mechanism or supported claim; omit invented differentiation.
- **Operating context:** workflows, environments, tools, and materials that affect use.
- **Capabilities and constraints:** actual functionality, terminology, integrations, technical boundaries, and undecided facts.
- **Brand commitments:** confirmed identity, voice, assets, or constraints future work must preserve.
- **Evidence on hand:** actual content, datasets, demonstrations, case studies, and assets, with useful source paths. Mark evidence that does not exist rather than fabricating it.
- **Product principles:** durable priorities grounded in confirmed requirements, not generic slogans or a prescribed count.
- **Accessibility and inclusion:** product-specific user needs and required standards when established.
- **Open decisions:** consequential missing facts and who or what can resolve them.

Do not fill absent sections with boilerplate. Keep session workflow settings, image-generation preferences, and page-specific composition choices out of this durable record.
