# Bolder

Make the selected surface more confident using the strongest parts of its existing design language. An amplification request does not by itself authorize a new identity.

## Find the source of flatness

Inspect the target, neighboring sections, tokens, content, and established components. Identify what the target underuses: display type, a signature motif, structural contrast, evidence, density, or pacing. Resolve the target from the request; ask only if materially ambiguous.

State the one change that will carry the emphasis and what should recede around it. Preserve claims and supplied assets; do not invent testimonials or metrics to make the composition convincing.

## Amplify

- Reuse established type, color, shape, and spacing roles at an appropriate intensity.
- Make one decisive compositional move. Increasing every element's weight removes hierarchy.
- Give the section a distinct rhythm relative to its neighbors through scale, density, or space.
- Use imagery or artifacts to carry relevant evidence, not as unrelated decoration.
- Check the structure without its copy: does grouping and emphasis still express the intended priority?

Keep changes inside the named scope. If a new system primitive or identity change is necessary, explain the concrete gap and resolve that choice only when existing authorization does not cover it.

## Verify

Compare before and after at relevant widths and states. Check that the focal point is clearer, the result still belongs to the same product, surrounding sections remain intact, and controls, keyboard focus, contrast, and motion preferences still work. Report the change and any untested states. Use [polish](../polish/SKILL.md) for a final pass when included in the task.
